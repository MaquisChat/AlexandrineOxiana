## How a lock reaches a reader
While a project is unlocked its data key sits in a key ring; a lock marks the project locked at once and wipes the key (zero-fills the buffer) as soon as no change is being saved. A READER that took the key before the lock and is still reading afterwards now gets a refusal from the opening primitives (`KeyNotAvailableError`: an all-zero key is "locked"). The remaining fault: readers that CATCH errors around an opening and turn them into something else — "damaged", "mismatched", "missing", a skipped line, a shorter list — and sometimes record a warning or write the short result back.
