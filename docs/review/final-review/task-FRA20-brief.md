## The defect (found by a reviewer with a probe; the review: `/home/user/AlexandrineOxiana/.superpowers/sdd/PLAN/task54-wb7-review.md`, finding I1 — read it)
When a project's rules say a photo's location must be removed ("strip") or made coarse ("coarsen"), the worker makes a PROXY copy of the photo without the location (`services/media-worker/ao_worker/image_proxy.py`, function `run`, about lines 113–150); that proxy is what exports and the screens then use.
